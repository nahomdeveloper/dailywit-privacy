DailyWit — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

DailyWit is a daily trivia quiz app developed by Nahom Labs. This policy explains what data the app collects and how it is used.

Information we collect

DailyWit does not require an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, or any other directly identifying personal information.

The app uses the following third-party services, which may collect limited, non-identifying technical data:

  • Firebase Analytics (Google): collects anonymous usage events (for example, quiz started, quiz completed, score and streak milestones) to help us understand how the app is used and improve it. These events carry an anonymous app instance identifier only not your name, Email, or any contact details.
  • Google AdMob: serves the optional rewarded ads used to unlock quizzes beyond the first three free ones. Google may collect device identifiers such as the advertising ID to serve and measure ads. You can reset or limit ad personalization in your device settings.

Data stored on your device

Your quiz progress, streaks, stars, settings, and reminder preferences are stored only on your device. They are never uploaded to our servers, because we operate none — DailyWit has no backend of its own.

Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with anyone, except for the anonymous analytics and advertising described above, which are governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

Children

DailyWit is a general-knowledge quiz suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a revised "Last updated" date.
